Restock Inventory decision assistant

Which SKUs to buy, and which to stop buying.

Upload an inventory export — Excel or CSV — and Restock works out the reorder point, safety stock and economic order quantity for every line, then tells you what to do about it: what to order today, what is drowning in cover, what stopped selling, and which supplier to call first. Every recommendation carries a quantity and a rupee figure.
